☕ TEACH ME HOW TO ESPRESSO ☕

Don't panic. It has approximately 900 controls and you're going to care about, like, four of them.

💖 THE CHEAT CODE

18g COFFEE → ~36g ESPRESSO → ~25–30 SEC

This is your starting point, not a sacred law. Use the double basket. A little kitchen scale under the cup makes learning approximately 900% less mysterious.

⚠️ IMPORTANT!!!
Change one thing at a time. Usually, start by adjusting the grind.

🫘 PLEASE PLEASE PLEASE

Please use reasonably fresh whole beans. Put them in the hopper, fill the water tank, and let the machine/portafilter get properly hot before you start.

For your first attempt, pick a middle-ish grinder setting. The exact number isn't magic because different beans behave differently.

☕ THAT'S THAT ME ESPRESSO

  1. Weigh about 18g of beans/grounds.
  2. Grind into the double basket.
  3. Level the grounds so they're reasonably even.
  4. Tamp firmly and level. You do not need to bench-press it.
  5. Lock in the portafilter, put your cup + scale underneath, and brew.
  6. Stop around 36g out. Aim for roughly 25–30 seconds as a starting point.

🚨 WHY DOES IT TASTE WEIRD?

Shot gushes out / tastes thin or sour?
→ Grind FINER.

Barely drips / machine seems to be fighting for its life?
→ Grind COARSER.

Tastes harsh, dry or unpleasantly bitter?
→ Try a slightly coarser grind / shorter extraction.

Actually tastes good?
→ STOP TOUCHING THINGS. You did it. ✨

🥛 BED CHEM

  1. Pour cold milk to around the bottom of the jug's spout.
  2. Turn steam on and purge the wand first.
  3. Put the tip just under the surface and introduce a little air: think soft tss-tss, not violent jacuzzi.
  4. Then sink the tip slightly and create a whirlpool until the jug is too hot to comfortably hold.
  5. Turn steam off before removing the wand.
  6. WIPE + PURGE THE WAND IMMEDIATELY. Dried milk is your enemy.

🧼 DON'T BE NASTY

After making coffee: knock out the puck, rinse the basket/portafilter, flush a little water through the group head, wipe the machine, and empty the drip tray when it starts getting gross.

When the machine asks for a cleaning cycle, believe her. She knows.
